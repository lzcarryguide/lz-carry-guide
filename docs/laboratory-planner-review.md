# Laboratory route planner — evidence and limitations

Checked: 2026-11-09

## What the tool does
- Lets a visitor select HQ and Laboratory levels.
- Provides route focuses for badge-light foundation, early-game F2P / low badge income, mid-game F2P / low badge income, Alliance Duel rewards, combat/survival, troop specialization, high badge income and late-game targets.
- Tracks each selected tree as Not started / In progress / Done in browser local storage.
- Keeps the second Laboratory level separate from the primary Lab level and asks the visitor to confirm the Season 2 Day 50 condition before treating Army Expansion as level-ready.
- Shows community-reported gates as hints; the suggested next item is a route focus, not an unlock prediction. The script checks selected Lab / HQ / second-Lab inputs only; prerequisite completion and percentage gates remain a manual in-game check.

## Evidence used
- SIN Command Research Priority: https://www.lastz.guide/progression/research-priority/ — 21-tree route, broad priorities and common gate map.
- Last Z Guide Laboratory: https://lastzguide.com/laboratory/ — Lab-level gates, second Laboratory context and the split reports for Siege to Seize.
- Z Tools Research Tree Unlocks: https://ztools.co.uk/wiki/research-tree-unlocks/ — Server 277 unlock audit, alternate Allied Troops entry, second-Lab and Season 2 Day 50 Army Expansion notes.
- Last Z Survival Resources: https://lastzsurvival.com/resources — indexed node-cost tables; exact totals are not promoted because current client/server applicability is not established.
- Last Z Exchange research-cost guide: https://lzexchange.net/guides/research-costs — cross-check of 21-tree labels and reported gates.

## Reconciliation and confidence
- Tree names and progression paths are community-indexed, not publisher-certified.
- Siege to Seize is reported at 15% by some current references and 40% by Z Tools' Server 277 audit.
- Elite Troops badge pricing differs between sources.
- Age of Steel / Steel Age label and Lab gate differ.
- Army Expansion is reported with Rapid Growth 50%, Lab No. 2 level 33 and Season 2 Day 50 by the Z Tools audit; other trackers simplify it to Lab 33 + Rapid Growth 50%. The planner explicitly requests Lab No. 2 and Season 2 Day 50 rather than silently treating primary Lab 33 as sufficient.
- Allied Troops appears in one alternate reference but not the current 21-tree list. It remains marked alternate / verify in the directory.
- The planner does not invent node costs, research times, badge totals, percentages, exact unlocks, or server-specific requirements.

## Planner wording and research-first layout — 2026-11-10
The Research Center now opens with the searchable technology directory before the Laboratory planner, matching the search-first requirement. The planner labels its suggestion as a "route focus — not an unlock prediction" and reports that meeting a Lab / HQ level gate does not prove the prerequisite tree is complete. Early-, mid-, late-game and badge-income profiles provide different starting routes without labelling them universally best or assuming spending level alone determines the correct choice.


## F2P / badge-income profile evidence — 2026-11-10
LastZ.GG's F2P progression guide (updated 9 Nov 2026) explicitly recommends investing badges in Alliance Recognition first and participating in Alliance Duel. The early-game low-badge route now reflects this as the first badge investment for active Duel participants, while preserving the Elite Troops badge-price conflict: check the live Laboratory before assuming it is free or badge-funded. This is a community strategy recommendation, not a universal publisher rule.
