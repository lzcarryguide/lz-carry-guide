# Lucky Rose verification and publication policy

## Current evidence snapshot

Reviewed 10 November 2026. LastZ.GG and Z Tools independently report the week beginning 9 November 2026 as Lucky Rose #5, +20% Research Speed, for two hours. Both pages show a 9 November update. This is a **Community-supported candidate**, not a LZ Carry in-game verification.

- LastZ.GG: https://lastz.gg/guides/lucky-rose
- Z Tools: https://ztools.co.uk/tools/lucky-rose/

Both pages also report the prior week (2 November) as #7, +10% Troop ATK, for two hours, and 26 October as #3, +30% Gathering Speed, for 24 hours. These are retained as community-supported history only.

## Unresolved scope conflict

LastZ.GG says the sender and their alliance receive the Lucky Rose buff. Z Tools and Last Z Guide describe the effect as sender-only. LZ Carry must not claim alliance-wide sharing without direct confirmation from the current game client. Until then, present the recipient scope as disputed and advise players to treat the buff as sender-only.

## Publication gate

A weekly number may be labelled **Verified** only when all of the following are recorded:

1. A current in-game observation for that exact week showing the lucky number, buff text and duration.
2. A second independent, current community report for the same week that agrees on number, effect and duration.
3. The observation date, week start, source links and any server/reset scope.
4. No unresolved conflict in the values being published.

If the in-game observation is missing, show the candidate as Community-supported and explicitly say awaiting in-game confirmation. If current sources disagree on number, effect or duration, show Unconfirmed and preserve both claims. Never infer a new week's number from a previous sequence.

## Data maintenance

- Keep `data/lucky-rose-history.json` append-only.
- Add a new week as a new record; do not overwrite historical observations.
- Corrections must be appended with a correction note and source evidence.
- A stale candidate must not be carried forward as the active week.
- Do not store or request account credentials, gift transactions or personal player data.
