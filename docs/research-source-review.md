# Research Guide source review

Checked: 2026-11-09.

## Publication approach
The public LZ Carry Research & Progression page is an original synthesis: a 21-tree directory, Laboratory gates, stage routes, badge-budget advice and resource/queue management. It does not copy third-party prose, layouts or artwork. Tree names and major gates are secondary-source findings, not official publisher data. The player's current Laboratory screen is authoritative after patches and server/season differences.

## Cross-reference findings
- Last Z Guide's research-priority and Laboratory pages describe a 21-tree reference, stage priorities and unlock routes.
- Last Z Exchange's research cost guide enumerates 21 trees and gives a Lab/prerequisite table.
- Z Tools has a separate research unlock article and interactive research tracker. Some labels differ: "Field" vs "Field Research"; its article mentions "Allied Troops" but the other current 21-tree list does not count it separately. LZ Carry excludes it from the current tree directory pending an in-game screenshot.
- The public 21 trees are: New Home, Shelter Building, Elite Troops, Hero Training, HQ Management, Military Strategies, Siege to Seize, Peace Shield, Rapid Growth, Field Research, Army Building, Alliance Recognition, Fully Armed Alliance, Unit Special Training, Army Expansion, Rider Training, Assaulter Training, Shooter Training, Age of Steel, Tactical Master and Leader's Will.

## Strategy findings
- Complete badge-light foundation and Elite Troops; continue Shelter Building → HQ Management → Rapid Growth.
- Alliance Recognition is an early badge priority for active Alliance Duel participants because its reported benefits accrue across recurring participation; less urgent for inactive alliance players.
- Military Strategies is a broad-combat route and unlock bridge to the Peace Shield / Siege to Seize fork.
- Urgent Rescue is repeatedly called out as a practical PvP-survival checkpoint; exact node location and effect still need a client screenshot.
- Siege to Seize completion opens Field; Field completion opens troop-specific branches. Specialize in the primary march's troop class rather than funding all three simultaneously.
- Unit Special Training is the T10 path and is reported to require HQ 30 + Lab 30. Full costs and timers are not published.
- Age of Steel is described as support-oriented; judge it against the account's construction, research, healing or support bottleneck.
- Army Expansion is badge-free but resource/time intensive; it is background work, not costless progression.
- Tactical Master and Leader's Will are major late-game commitments. Leader's Will is role-dependent.
- Alliance Duel day order and point values differ across published schedules. The public guide directs players to the live task panel and does not publish universal weekday scoring.
- Exact node bonuses, full effects, badge costs, resource totals, speeds and timers remain unpublished because they were not checked against current in-game Lab panels.

## Discrepancies / evidence backlog

2. "Field" vs "Field Research" naming varies.

4. Second Laboratory / Research License availability and effects may be season/server/offer-sensitive.
5. Research node order, Urgent Rescue position and all node-level values require current Lab tree panels.
6. HQ level is not interchangeable with Lab level; Unit Special Training is reported to have both HQ 30 and Lab 30 gates.

## Additional cross-check on 2026-11-09

A current source re-check found specific differences that are now shown in the public guide as unresolved:
- SIN Command's current Research Priority tree table lists New Home at Lab 1, Shelter Building at Lab 5, and Elite Troops / Hero Training at Lab 8.
- Last Z Exchange's current research-cost table likewise lists New Home Lab 1, Shelter Building Lab 5 and Elite Troops Lab 8.
- Last Z Guide's recently crawled Laboratory guide describes the initial free trees (New Home, Shelter Building and Elite Troops) as being available when the Laboratory opens and emphasizes prior-tree completion for the free chain. It also explains Lab levels are research-tree gates and the building does not directly increase research speed; research speed is affected by separate bonuses and nodes.
- HQ Management's separate Lab 14 gate is listed in the Last Z Exchange table but the newer Last Z Guide research page presents Shelter Building 100% as the unlock, without repeating a separate Lab 14 gate.
- Siege to Seize is listed at Military Strategies 15% by Last Z Exchange / SIN Command; Last Z Guide's Laboratory text reports a 15–40% split among trackers.
- Army Expansion is listed at a Lab 33 gate by Last Z Exchange and Last Z Guide; Z Tools' source notes also mention a Lab No. 2 level 33. The current client must resolve whether the requirement is Lab 1 or Lab No. 2.
- Last Z Guide's Laboratory page describes a second Laboratory unlocked around HQ 18 with a Research License; acquisition source and availability differ among community references.

The public unlock table now marks these discrepancies and expressly tells readers to confirm the active gate in their game client. It does not publish exact badge costs or node percentages from these sources.
