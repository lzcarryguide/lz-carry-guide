Research & Progression

A breakpoint-first research plan. Learn which tree to open next, why it matters, when to pause, and how to protect badges, resources and speedups.

The LZ Carry rule

Research toward a milestone, not a completion bar

01 · Foundation

Finish the badge-light base

Advance New Home, Shelter Building and Elite Troops. These foundational paths support account growth and open later research branches without competing for the scarce badge budget.

02 · Recurring value

Make alliance research earn back

Alliance Recognition is an early badge priority for active Alliance Duel players. Its reported value compounds across recurring participation; it is less urgent if you rarely play alliance events.

03 · Combat bridge

Secure broad combat and survival

Finish Elite Troops, open Military Strategies, then reach the Peace Shield breakpoint for Urgent Rescue. Do not confuse an expensive full-tree completion with reaching the one useful node you need.

04 · Specialize

Choose one main troop line

After Field opens the dedicated troop trees, prioritize the class that supports your main formation. Spreading badges across Riders, Assaulters and Shooters delays meaningful progress.

Progression stages

HQ and Laboratory priorities by stage

Early · Lab 1–10

Build your foundation

  • Advance New Home and Shelter Building. Do not neglect badge-free account development.
  • Open Elite Troops at its Lab gate and aim for 100% to unlock Military Strategies.
  • At Lab 8, review Hero Training's useful early checkpoint, but do not let it displace every other priority.
  • At Lab 10, New Home 50% reportedly opens Alliance Recognition and Fully Armed Alliance. Favor Recognition when you actively join Alliance Duel.
  • Join an active alliance early enough to benefit from alliance research and shared event planning.

Next: pursue the next account bottleneck and preserve badges for Alliance Recognition and broad combat.

Mid-game · Lab 11–24

Open the economy and combat gates

  • Complete Shelter Building to open HQ Management; HQ Management 100% reportedly opens Rapid Growth at Lab 22.
  • Complete Elite Troops to open Military Strategies at Lab 16.
  • Military Strategies reportedly opens Siege to Seize at 15% and Peace Shield at 45%.
  • Target Urgent Rescue in Peace Shield when PvP troop survival is the concern. Confirm its node position and live effect first.
  • Complete Siege to Seize when Field access and a later troop-specialization branch are your next goal.

Next: choose Field access or Peace Shield survival based on your actual account needs.

Advanced · Lab 25–29

Build breadth without losing focus

  • Field Research 100% reportedly opens Rider, Assaulter and Shooter Training at Lab 25.
  • Peace Shield 100% reportedly opens Army Building at Lab 25.
  • Pick a class tree that matches your primary deployed march; do not fund all three at once on a limited badge income.
  • Fully Armed Alliance is situational: pursue it for a specific verified war node or alliance role, not by default.
  • Keep HQ and Laboratory progression aligned if T10 is a long-term goal.

Next: select a single troop specialization, Army Building, or an approaching HQ/Lab milestone.

Late-game · Lab 30+

Commit to a sustainable endgame

  • Unit Special Training is the T10 route; current community references report both HQ 30 and Lab 30 requirements.
  • Age of Steel is described as support-oriented. Consider it when base development, research, healing or support efficiency is your bottleneck.
  • Army Building 100% reportedly opens Tactical Master. Treat this as a major badge commitment.
  • Leader's Will is an ultra-late, leadership-oriented route, not a routine target for most accounts.
  • Army Expansion is described as badge-free but highly resource/time intensive, with a separate second-Lab gate to verify.

Next: choose the tree that fits your main march, alliance role, badge income and live unlocks. Community priority lists commonly put Alliance Recognition, Elite Troops, Military Strategies to the Peace Shield gate, Urgent Rescue, and a Hero Training checkpoint ahead of high-cost endgame completion. Some recommend Urgent Rescue at Level 10; verify the in-game cap and effect rather than treating a third-party target as universal.

Foundation · No badges reported

New Home

Early account development and a prerequisite for alliance branches.

Reported gate: Lab build / Laboratory 1 (Z Tools says the tree is available when the Laboratory is first built; SIN Command lists Lab 1).

Why: Establishes the first progression route and is a prerequisite for Alliance Recognition and Fully Armed Alliance.

Priority: High early. Complete the foundation rather than leaving it unfinished to chase a badge branch.

Can wait? It is not a long-term optional tree. Verify the next unlock shown by your Lab panel.

Foundation · No badges reported

Shelter Building

Shelter development and the HQ Management prerequisite chain.

Reported gate: Lab build (Z Tools) / Laboratory 5 (SIN Command). The tree's completion is the prerequisite for HQ Management.

Why: Completing it reportedly opens HQ Management in the later free-research chain.

Priority: High when available; it is reliable non-badge progress while badge research is constrained.

Can wait? Avoid leaving it incomplete if HQ Management and Rapid Growth are in your plan.

Core combat · badge price disputed

Elite Troops

Early troop development; opens Military Strategies.

Reported gate: Lab built (available from start in Z Tools); Last Z Guide / SIN Command lists Lab 8.

Badge cost: disputed. Z Tools (26 May 2026, Server 277) lists this as badge-paid; SIN Command (September 2026 snapshot) lists no badge cost. Check the live node price before budgeting.

Why: Completion at 100% reportedly opens Military Strategies.

Priority: High if the live badge cost fits your income. It is a critical prerequisite gate.

Can wait? Do not stop just short of 100% when Military Strategies is your next target.

Parallel combat · badge tree

Allied Troops

A separate early badge tree running beside Elite Troops.

Reported gate: Lab built / available from start (Z Tools, 26 May 2026, Server 277).

Why: Z Tools lists Allied Troops as a separate badge-paid tree and says no later tree requires it. It is omitted from the 21-tree SIN Command list.

Priority: Low to situational. Treat it as a secondary stat project after Hero Training's Cockpit checkpoint, Alliance Recognition, Military Strategies and the Peace Shield survival target.

Can wait? Yes. Defer it until the important unlock chain is secure and your live Laboratory confirms the tree exists.

Hero development · Badge tree

Hero Training

Hero-focused combat development and an early checkpoint.

Reported gate: early access with no documented tree prerequisite in Z Tools (typically HQ 13–15); SIN Command lists Lab 8.

Why: The tree provides hero ATK / DEF improvements. Current priority guides point to the Cockpit checkpoint as the efficient early stopping point.

Priority: Reach the Cockpit checkpoint, then pause. SIN Command advises against maxing the full tree early because deeper badge spending has weaker return.

Can wait? Yes. Resume later when core alliance and combat unlocks are secure and the live node benefit justifies its badge cost.

Foundation · No badges reported

HQ Management

HQ development and resource-cap progression.

Reported gate: Shelter Building 100%; SIN Command adds Lab 14, while the Z Tools tracker does not show a separate Lab 14 gate.

Why: Community references describe HQ/resource development benefits. Completion reportedly opens Rapid Growth.

Priority: High for accounts limited by HQ progression. It is useful background research when badges are scarce.

Can wait? Keep it moving when your resources and queue allow; do not rush it with premium speedups merely to finish a bar.

Combat · Badge tree

Military Strategies

Broad troop development and the Peace Shield / Siege to Seize fork.

Reported gate: Elite Troops 100%; the community unlock trackers differ on the separate Lab level (SIN Command lists Lab 16, Z Tools emphasizes the tree-completion prerequisite).

Why: Cross-referenced guides describe broad troop, field and siege improvements. 15% reportedly opens Siege to Seize; 45% opens Peace Shield.

Priority: High, as a broad-combat route and unlock bridge.

Can wait? Beyond 15% and 45%, choose nodes that serve your next target instead of chasing the highest completion percentage.

Offense · Badge tree

Siege to Seize

Siege, rally and offensive path to Field Research.

Reported gate: Laboratory 18 plus Military Strategies at 15% (SIN Command) or 40% (Z Tools, Server 277). Confirm the exact threshold on your server.

Why: Full completion reportedly opens Field Research, the gateway to troop-class trees.

Priority: High when troop specialization is your next major milestone.

Can wait? Pause after the required unlock if Peace Shield's survival value is more urgent.

Survival · Badge tree

Peace Shield

Defensive development; Urgent Rescue checkpoint and Army Building gate.

Reported gate: Laboratory 18 + Military Strategies at 45%.

Why: Urgent Rescue is repeatedly highlighted as a practical PvP survival target. Full completion reportedly opens Army Building.

Priority: High for accounts exposed to PvP or preparing for higher-tier combat.

Can wait? Do not max every branch before confirming Urgent Rescue's exact position and live effect.

Economy · No badges reported

Rapid Growth

Later account development in the badge-free chain.

Reported gate: HQ Management 100%; Lab 22 is reported by SIN Command. Other trackers emphasize the tree-completion requirement without the same separate Lab gate.

Why: Continues non-badge progression and is cited as a prerequisite for Army Expansion.

Priority: High when resources or HQ development are the bottleneck; useful parallel research when badges are reserved for combat.

Can wait? Avoid accelerating long nodes at the expense of a near-term combat breakpoint.

Field · Badge tree

Field Research / Field

Intermediate toolkit and gateway to troop-specific research.

Reported gate: Siege to Seize 100%; Lab 22 is listed by SIN Command. Confirm whether your current server shows a separate Lab 22 gate.

Why: Completion reportedly opens Rider, Assaulter and Shooter Training. A late node named Recharge Shield appears in community references; verify its path in-game.

Priority: High when class specialization is the next goal. Plan the complete badge commitment before starting.

Formation development and Tactical Master prerequisite.

Why: Community guides describe formation-wide development. Completion reportedly opens Tactical Master.

Priority: Strong long-term route after urgent survival and Field needs; compare node value with badge cost in your client.

Alliance economy · Badge tree

Reported gate: no separate prerequisite documented in Z Tools; SIN Command lists Lab 10 + New Home 50%.

Why: Current community guides rank its recurring Alliance Duel reward value highly for active alliance members.

Priority: One of the strongest early badge targets if you participate consistently in Alliance Duel.

Can wait? Temporarily, if an immediate survival or prerequisite gate is more urgent. Check the live task list; theme names do not guarantee that every action scores.

War · Badge tree

Fully Armed Alliance

Alliance war and combat-focused research.

Reported gate: no separate prerequisite documented in Z Tools; SIN Command lists Lab 10 + New Home 50%.

Why: Described as war-oriented, rather than a recurring rewards engine like Alliance Recognition.

Priority: Situational: pick it for an identified war role, alliance plan or verified node.

Can wait? Commonly postponed by F2P and low-spend players while Recognition, Military Strategies and Urgent Rescue remain unfinished.

T10 route · Badge tree

Unit Special Training

Long-term research path to T10 troops.

Reported gate: HQ 30 and Laboratory 30.

Why: This is the T10 route, but it is a multi-stage project involving badges, electricity, other resources and substantial time.

Priority: High only if T10 is a deliberate target and your badge/resource income can sustain it.

Can wait? Yes. Rushing T10 may crowd out broad combat, survival or specialization that improves the account sooner.

HQ and Laboratory coordination

HQ checkpoints that affect research planning

Most research availability is gated by Laboratory level and earlier-tree completion, not HQ level alone. Only treat HQ gates as requirements when a current source or your client shows one.

HQ 13–15 · Source-dependent

Hero Training access

Z Tools says Hero Training has no documented tree prerequisite and typically appears around HQ 13–15. SIN Command lists it at Lab 8. Check your unlock screen before reserving badges or changing the early plan.

HQ 18 · Second queue

Laboratory No. 2

Z Tools reports the second Laboratory unlocks around HQ 18. It describes a Research License bought from the Alliance Shop as the item that opens a second concurrent research queue. The license source, cost and availability may change; verify your live shop and Lab before planning parallel projects.

HQ 30 + Lab 30

Unit Special Training / T10

Current community research references report that Unit Special Training requires both HQ 30 and Laboratory 30. This is the clearest HQ-specific endgame research gate in this source set. Do not confuse the HQ level with the Lab level.

Planning rule: upgrade HQ when the next HQ unlock, building prerequisite or T10 target justifies it. Upgrade the Laboratory when a useful research tree gate is near. Do not assume raising HQ alone makes research faster; separate research-speed bonuses and active Lab nodes are a different system.

Upgrade toward the next useful gate

Compare Lab construction requirements and prerequisite buildings with the value of the tree it unlocks. Keep HQ progression aligned when a later branch has an HQ-level gate.

Use parallel queues deliberately

Some current guides describe a second Laboratory / Research License as a separate queue. A recent Laboratory guide reports it unlocks at HQ 18, while other indexes differ on purchase source and availability. Confirm the live Research License description, price and prerequisites before planning around it. A practical split is badge research in one queue and resource-funded research in another.

Know which timer you are accelerating

Laboratory construction, research-node timers and troop training are separate systems. Check the applicable speed bonus and event panel before using a speedup.

Protect the account budget

Resources, badges and speedups

Badges are scarce

Identify the exact node or unlock before entering a badge tree. Compare the remaining badge cost displayed by the client with alternative research targets. Badge-free does not mean resource-free.

Synchronize with real event tasks

Alliance Duel scoring and related event tasks can differ by server and cycle. Check the current point panel before spending badges, speedups, hero items or training resources. Do not assume fixed weekday scoring.

Keep queues useful

If a second queue is active, avoid running two expensive badge trees simultaneously when badge income is the bottleneck. Resource-funded research can make useful background progress.

Save flexible speedups

Hold flexible speedups for a confirmed scoring window or meaningful unlock. Do not spend premium items simply to rush a low-impact node or to make a tree look complete.

Budget the whole project

Consider badges, electricity, Zents, other materials, research time and the opportunity cost of pausing another branch. A long project is a commitment, not just a node list.

Do not stall natural growth

Keep HQ progression, production and useful queues moving. Event points are valuable only when their reward outweighs the cost of delaying a stronger account milestone.

Match your spending level

F2P, low-spending and high-spending routes

F2P

Protect every badge

  1. Advance New Home, Shelter Building and Elite Troops.
  2. Prioritize Alliance Recognition if you play Alliance Duel consistently.
  3. Reach Military Strategies' branch gates and secure Urgent Rescue for survival.
  4. Use Hero Training's practical checkpoint; return later if needed.
  5. Choose only one class tree after Field. Postpone Fully Armed Alliance and major endgame branches unless a specific node is worth it.

Measure success: main march strength, alliance rewards and survivability—not matching a spender's tree completion.

Low spend

Buy time, not random completion

  1. Follow the same foundation, alliance and combat priorities as F2P.
  2. Evaluate a second queue only after confirming its availability and actual value on your account.
  3. Use parallel research to keep non-badge progress running while badges accrue.
  4. Choose one class specialization and one major endgame objective at a time.
  5. Reserve paid speedups or badge sources for a planned milestone, not an impulse buy.

Measure success: queue uptime and predictable milestone progress without stalling many expensive branches.

High spend / high badge income

Parallelize without skipping foundations

  1. Finish Alliance Recognition, broad combat and Urgent Rescue.
  2. Open Field and Army Building as gates become available.
  3. Start Unit Special Training once HQ/Lab requirements are confirmed if T10 is the chosen goal.
  4. Invest in the troop class used by the main formation.
  5. Evaluate Tactical Master, Age of Steel and Leader's Will by alliance role and account bottleneck.
  6. Run Army Expansion in the background if its second-Lab gate is met.

Measure success: a few funded projects reaching useful breakpoints, not many stalled trees.

Before you research

Account-development checklist

A strong account does not need every research bar filled at the same time. It needs the right prerequisites, useful combat breakpoints and a route the player can sustain.
