A practical progression guide

Use this sequence to build a reliable account routine. The advice focuses on decision-making and safe planning; it does not assume one universal server schedule, upgrade price or hero ranking.

1. Secure your account and learn your serverStart with access and account safety
  • Confirm that your account is bound to a recovery method you control and that you can sign in after changing devices.
  • Learn your server, alliance, account age and any server-specific restrictions that affect events or migration.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes, recovery links or account-transfer credentials with a guide site or another player.
  • Keep a private note of your own account milestones; LZ Carry does not connect to or read your game account.
2. Plan HQ upgrades before spendingCurrent level → target level → prerequisites
  • Record your current HQ level in the Player Dashboard. The value is player-entered and can be changed whenever your account advances.
  • Open the HQ Planner and select a realistic next target. Review every prerequisite building and mark only the requirements you have personally completed.
  • Compare published community figures with the resource and time values shown by your own live upgrade panel. Discounts, server rules and game updates can change the result.
  • Include prerequisite-building costs in your personal budget; the planner's HQ totals do not automatically include every building upgrade.
  • Do not spend resources based only on a screenshot or an old guide. Your in-game upgrade panel is authoritative for your account.
Open the HQ Upgrade Planner
3. Build a sustainable Laboratory routineChoose useful branches and verify gates
  • Read the unlock condition on the Laboratory node itself before planning a long research chain. Community sources sometimes disagree on Lab levels, badge prices or completion percentages.
  • Prioritize foundation and account-wide improvements that support your current goal. Avoid starting several expensive badge branches at once if your badge income cannot sustain them.
  • Use the Research Planner to record your own state. Its route suggestions are planning aids, not a live unlock detector.
  • Copy the cost from the current in-game research panel into the Research Resource Calculator. The calculator compares your entered resources with your entered cost; it does not supply game prices.
  • Keep a note of disputed gates and revisit them after updates or when your live tree shows a different requirement.
Open Research Center
4. Develop a focused hero formationBuild around a role you can actually support
  • Choose one main formation to develop before spreading scarce fragments, manuals, experience or equipment across many heroes.
  • Use the Formation Builder to compare faction and troop-line fit, but check the actual hero skill text, targets, triggers and restrictions in your client.
  • Do not assume rarity alone determines a hero's role or long-term value. A role label can be community-supported while an individual skill or season detail remains disputed.
  • Save a named formation before experimenting. You can compare saved lineups and export a backup using My Progress.
  • Keep hero investment decisions flexible as new heroes, awakenings and equipment become available on your server.
Open Hero Formation Lab
5. Track troop goals without guessing costsSeparate quantities from unlock and promotion rules
  • Record your own current and target unit quantities in the Troop Target Planner.
  • The displayed difference is a quantity gap only. It does not infer training costs, timers, unlock levels or lower-tier promotion ratios.
  • Check troop class, tier, camp and research requirements in your live client before committing resources.
  • Use the dashboard to revisit your saved target as your march develops.
Open Troop Target Planner
6. Prepare for events from the live rulesDo not assume a fixed rotation
  • Open the active event panel and read eligibility, task list, scoring, caps, duration and reward milestones before preparing.
  • Save relevant resources only when the current event rules confirm that the action will count. Old score tables and calendar screenshots may be stale.
  • Separate personal milestones from alliance-wide contribution. Coordinate with your alliance when the live event requires it.
  • Use the Event Preparation checklist to record your plan. Event dates and rotations are not automatically synchronized by LZ Carry.
Open Events Center
Review your plan regularly. Recheck the live game after updates, new seasons and server changes. Community-supported information is a useful starting point, not a guarantee that a value or unlock is current for your account.
