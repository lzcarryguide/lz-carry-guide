Assaulters

Blood Rose

Seasonal core to compare: Selena (S1) → Bella (S2) + Licia (S3) → Yu Chan (S4). Season 5 adds red-star awakening for Oliveira (S1), not a new hero. Selena contributes Assaulter-focused buffs; Bella and Licia are a reported seasonal pair; Yu Chan is the Season 4 damage carry in current community guides.

Strength: focused Assaulter damage and faction synergy.

Trade-off: the counter triangle makes matchup and server meta matter.

Alternatives: build the strongest verified Blood Rose heroes you already developed; don't replace a starred core with an unbuilt seasonal hero automatically.

Shooters

Wings of Dawn

Seasonal core to compare: Scarlett / Nyx (S1) → Alma (S2) + Liliana (S3) → Queenie (S4). Season 5 adds red-star awakening for Scarlett (S1), not a new hero. The Alma–Liliana pairing is repeatedly cited in formation references; Queenie is the Season 4 carry in current community guides.

Strength: Shooter-focused damage and a strong option into Blood Rose.

Trade-off: check skill scope and whether your server opponents have shifted factions.

Alternatives: use verified base Shooter heroes while building seasonal pairs.

Riders

Guard of Order

Seasonal core to compare: Sakura / Evelyn (S1) → Harleyna (S2) + Dodemeki (S3) → Amber (S4). Season 5 adds red-star awakening for Evelyn (S1), not a new hero. The Harleyna–Dodemeki pair is a reported seasonal combination; Amber is the Season 4 carry in current community guides.

Strength: Rider alignment and counter pressure against Wings of Dawn.

Trade-off: avoid spreading scarce upgrades across all three factions.

Alternatives: use the best developed verified Guard of Order heroes available to you.

Faction counter model in community guides: Wings of Dawn over Blood Rose; Guard of Order over Wings of Dawn; Blood Rose over Guard of Order. It is a matchup modifier, not a guaranteed win. Test in the same mode with comparable troops and buffs.
