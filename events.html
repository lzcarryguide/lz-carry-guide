Player routine

Daily and weekly event routine

Daily

Before collecting or spending

  • Open Event Center and check active task, reset, countdown and eligibility.
  • Check Full Preparedness and Alliance Duel separately; overlapping themes do not guarantee identical scoring.
  • Collect free attempts and routine rewards before expiry, but check whether claiming scores before delaying it.
  • Keep natural building, research and training moving if delays waste production or queue time.
Weekly

Plan the alliance cycle

  • Read the current Alliance Duel overview and daily task tabs, not a fixed weekday chart.
  • Ask leadership about registration, rally, defense and participation for team events.
  • Set personal and alliance targets that fit your resource budget.
  • Claim earned rewards and update the saved-item plan for the next cycle.
F2P

Maximize useful rewards

  • Prioritize free attempts, routine tasks and personal milestones before leaderboard competition.
  • Save flexible items for actions the current panel confirms will score.
  • Coordinate shared objectives without unnecessary duplicate spending.
  • Stop when the next milestone needs disproportionate premium resources.
Low / high spend

Spend against verified value

  • Check prize track, threshold, currency and carry-over before buying.
  • Do not chase rank without knowing the score gap and reward difference.
  • Coordinate teleports, healing and buffs with leadership before alliance events.
  • Do not sacrifice account-defining progression for a temporary rank without a worthwhile reward.

Schedule rule: recurring does not mean universal. Follow the displayed server time, cycle, registration and reset. No fixed weekday rotation or clock conversion is published without current server-specific evidence.

Personal chest milestones

Each Full Preparedness rotation has three personal task chests. Use the live panel for exact point thresholds; Preparedness Medals also feed the daily medal-chest track.

Reward strategy: secure the personal chests you can reach efficiently before chasing leaderboard placement. Check the live thresholds, claim each chest when reached, and stop when the next reward is not worth the resources. Exact point thresholds and reward quantities are intentionally not hard-coded.

Your checklist is saved locally in this browser and rolls to a new AT date at reset. It does not submit game actions or send reminders.

Important: Full Preparedness rotates through six four-hour blocks, but the exact theme sequence and eligible actions must be read from your live Event Center. A related Alliance Duel theme does not mean every action scores in both events.

Six four-hour Full Prep rotations

A current guide describes theme changes at 00:00, 04:00, 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 Apocalypse Time. It identifies 08:00–12:00 and 20:00–00:00 as two separate windows that can match that day's Alliance Duel theme. Confirm the displayed AT and the active task list before delaying a claim or spending an item.

One action can score twice—but only when both lists agree

Full Preparedness and Alliance Duel themes can overlap. The exact eligible actions are not identical every time. Check both point panels; a matching theme label is not proof that every related action scores in both systems.

Hot Event cycle conflict: Z Tools describes a four-week cycle (Gacha Go, Bullseye/Booty, Lucky Discounter, Lucky Chest). Last Z Wiki's calendar describes a three-week cycle (Gacha Go, Shooting Loot, Lucky Discounter) with other activities alongside it. Treat both as community models, not a schedule promise. Open Events in your own game to identify the active event and check whether each currency carries over.

Preparation checklist

Before you spend or enter

Read the live task list

Confirm which actions count today, whether points are personal or alliance-wide, and whether a task has a cap or special condition.

Check your alliance plan

For team events, confirm registration, assigned roles, rally or reinforcement expectations, and the event start time with alliance leadership.

Protect your reserves

Keep enough healing, training, and upgrade resources for normal progression. Do not spend premium currency or rare items based only on an old event guide.
