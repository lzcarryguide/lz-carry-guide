The LZ Carry rule

Research toward a milestone, not a completion bar

01 · Foundation

Finish the badge-light base

Advance New Home, Shelter Building and Elite Troops. These foundational paths support account growth and open later research branches without competing for the scarce badge budget.

02 · Recurring value

Make alliance research earn back

Alliance Recognition is an early badge priority for active Alliance Duel players. Its reported value compounds across recurring participation; it is less urgent if you rarely play alliance events.

03 · Combat bridge

Secure broad combat and survival

Finish Elite Troops, open Military Strategies, then reach the Peace Shield breakpoint for Urgent Rescue. Do not confuse an expensive full-tree completion with reaching the one useful node you need.

04 · Specialize

Choose one main troop line

After Field opens the dedicated troop trees, prioritize the class that supports your main formation. Spreading badges across Riders, Assaulters and Shooters delays meaningful progress.

Progression stages

HQ and Laboratory priorities by stage

Early · Lab 1–10

Build your foundation

  • Advance New Home and Shelter Building. Do not neglect badge-free account development.
  • Open Elite Troops at its Lab gate and aim for 100% to unlock Military Strategies.
  • At Lab 8, review Hero Training's useful early checkpoint, but do not let it displace every other priority.
  • At Lab 10, New Home 50% reportedly opens Alliance Recognition and Fully Armed Alliance. Favor Recognition when you actively join Alliance Duel.
  • Join an active alliance early enough to benefit from alliance research and shared event planning.

Next: pursue the next account bottleneck and preserve badges for Alliance Recognition and broad combat.

Mid-game · Lab 11–24

Open the economy and combat gates

  • Complete Shelter Building to open HQ Management; HQ Management 100% reportedly opens Rapid Growth at Lab 22.
  • Complete Elite Troops to open Military Strategies at Lab 16.
  • Military Strategies reportedly opens Siege to Seize at 15% and Peace Shield at 45%.
  • Target Urgent Rescue in Peace Shield when PvP troop survival is the concern. Confirm its node position and live effect first.
  • Complete Siege to Seize when Field access and a later troop-specialization branch are your next goal.

Next: choose Field access or Peace Shield survival based on your actual account needs.

Advanced · Lab 25–29

Build breadth without losing focus

  • Field Research 100% reportedly opens Rider, Assaulter and Shooter Training at Lab 25.
  • Peace Shield 100% reportedly opens Army Building at Lab 25.
  • Pick a class tree that matches your primary deployed march; do not fund all three at once on a limited badge income.
  • Fully Armed Alliance is situational: pursue it for a specific verified war node or alliance role, not by default.
  • Keep HQ and Laboratory progression aligned if T10 is a long-term goal.

Next: select a single troop specialization, Army Building, or an approaching HQ/Lab milestone.

Late-game · Lab 30+

Commit to a sustainable endgame

  • Unit Special Training is the T10 route; current community references report both HQ 30 and Lab 30 requirements.
  • Age of Steel is described as support-oriented. Consider it when base development, research, healing or support efficiency is your bottleneck.
  • Army Building 100% reportedly opens Tactical Master. Treat this as a major badge commitment.
  • Leader's Will is an ultra-late, leadership-oriented route, not a routine target for most accounts.
  • Army Expansion is described as badge-free but highly resource/time intensive, with a separate second-Lab gate to verify.

Next: choose the tree that fits your main march, alliance role, badge income and live unlocks. Community priority lists commonly put Alliance Recognition, Elite Troops, Military Strategies to the Peace Shield gate, Urgent Rescue, and a Hero Training checkpoint ahead of high-cost endgame completion. Some recommend Urgent Rescue at Level 10; verify the in-game cap and effect rather than treating a third-party target as universal.

Upgrade toward the next useful gate

Compare Lab construction requirements and prerequisite buildings with the value of the tree it unlocks. Keep HQ progression aligned when a later branch has an HQ-level gate.

Use parallel queues deliberately

Some current guides describe a second Laboratory / Research License as a separate queue. A recent Laboratory guide reports it unlocks at HQ 18, while other indexes differ on purchase source and availability. Confirm the live Research License description, price and prerequisites before planning around it. A practical split is badge research in one queue and resource-funded research in another.

Know which timer you are accelerating

Laboratory construction, research-node timers and troop training are separate systems. Check the applicable speed bonus and event panel before using a speedup.

Protect the account budget

Resources, badges and speedups

Badges are scarce

Identify the exact node or unlock before entering a badge tree. Compare the remaining badge cost displayed by the client with alternative research targets. Badge-free does not mean resource-free.

Synchronize with real event tasks

Alliance Duel scoring and related event tasks can differ by server and cycle. Check the current point panel before spending badges, speedups, hero items or training resources. Do not assume fixed weekday scoring.

Keep queues useful

If a second queue is active, avoid running two expensive badge trees simultaneously when badge income is the bottleneck. Resource-funded research can make useful background progress.

Save flexible speedups

Hold flexible speedups for a confirmed scoring window or meaningful unlock. Do not spend premium items simply to rush a low-impact node or to make a tree look complete.

Budget the whole project

Consider badges, electricity, Zents, other materials, research time and the opportunity cost of pausing another branch. A long project is a commitment, not just a node list.

Do not stall natural growth

Keep HQ progression, production and useful queues moving. Event points are valuable only when their reward outweighs the cost of delaying a stronger account milestone.

Match your spending level

F2P, low-spending and high-spending routes

F2P

Protect every badge

  1. Advance New Home, Shelter Building and Elite Troops.
  2. Prioritize Alliance Recognition if you play Alliance Duel consistently.
  3. Reach Military Strategies' branch gates and secure Urgent Rescue for survival.
  4. Use Hero Training's practical checkpoint; return later if needed.
  5. Choose only one class tree after Field. Postpone Fully Armed Alliance and major endgame branches unless a specific node is worth it.

Measure success: main march strength, alliance rewards and survivability—not matching a spender's tree completion.

Low spend

Buy time, not random completion

  1. Follow the same foundation, alliance and combat priorities as F2P.
  2. Evaluate a second queue only after confirming its availability and actual value on your account.
  3. Use parallel research to keep non-badge progress running while badges accrue.
  4. Choose one class specialization and one major endgame objective at a time.
  5. Reserve paid speedups or badge sources for a planned milestone, not an impulse buy.

Measure success: queue uptime and predictable milestone progress without stalling many expensive branches.

High spend / high badge income

Parallelize without skipping foundations

  1. Finish Alliance Recognition, broad combat and Urgent Rescue.
  2. Open Field and Army Building as gates become available.
  3. Start Unit Special Training once HQ/Lab requirements are confirmed if T10 is the chosen goal.
  4. Invest in the troop class used by the main formation.
  5. Evaluate Tactical Master, Age of Steel and Leader's Will by alliance role and account bottleneck.
  6. Run Army Expansion in the background if its second-Lab gate is met.

Measure success: a few funded projects reaching useful breakpoints, not many stalled trees.

Before you research

Account-development checklist

A strong account does not need every research bar filled at the same time. It needs the right prerequisites, useful combat breakpoints and a route the player can sustain.
