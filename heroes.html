LZ Carry field notes

Formation and investment basics

Build around one main squad

Concentrate scarce hero experience, fragments, skill materials, and equipment on a primary formation before spreading upgrades across every available hero.

Read the skill, not just the power

Check who a skill affects, its troop-type restriction, activation condition, and duration. A higher power number alone does not prove a better fit for your march.

Separate training from promotion

Training creates units; promotion upgrades existing units where the game permits it. Review the selected camp and live confirmation panel before committing resources.
